# How to make an archive

Tags: archive-manual, starter, how-to, beginners
Source: Starter · `Starter.uXu.0003`

## What this room is for

**Starter** is the public practice archive. Anyone can open it, copy it, and learn the pattern.  
It stays this way on purpose so future users always have a clear example.

If someone has a **personal** archive (private room), that’s different — Starter is not that.

## Three files

| File | Job |
|------|-----|
| `index.html` | The page people open |
| `data.json` | Your name, blurb, and list |
| `README.md` | A short story for humans |

Optional: this manual, so **INDEX ARCHIVE MANUALS** can find you.

## Steps

1. On 0?0: `OPEN STARTER` — look around.
2. Copy `archives/Starter/` to `archives/Your-Name/`.
3. Change `archiveName` and `description` in `data.json`.
4. Replace the practice item under `shows` (or use `[]`).
5. Open your `index.html` in a browser.
6. Register: PR, or email **rasip@chloreform.org**.

## From 0?0

```text
OPEN STARTER
CREATE ARCHIVE
ARCHIVE MANUALS
```

## Keep it simple

Tier 1 is enough. Search, custom players, and provenance can wait until you need them.
